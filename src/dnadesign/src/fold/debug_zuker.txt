## 1. **`stack(...)`: Dangling‐ends fallback**  

**Lines ~16–20** (in the `for _, idx in ipairs(...) do` loop), you do:

```lua

    return energies.delta_g(fold_energy.enthalpyH, fold_energy.entropyS, foldContext.temp)
```

If `fold_energy` is **nil** (the key is missing from `danglingEnds`), you explicitly return an enthalpy=0, entropy=−1.6 fallback. That is an “improper zero” or “hard-coded fallback.”

1. **`stack`:**
